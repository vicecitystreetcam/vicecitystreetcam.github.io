Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the "Vice City Street Cam Uploader" application (the "App") handles information. The App is a private, single-user tool operated by the owner of the Vice City Street Cam YouTube channel to upload and schedule the channel's own videos.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the App accesses

  • An OAuth access token for the channel owner's own Google account, used only to upload videos, set their metadata (title, description, tags, schedule) and read basic statistics of the channel's own videos.
  • Public metadata of publicly available videos and clips (titles, view counts, creator names, links), used to choose source material and to credit original creators.

How information is used and stored

All data is stored locally on the owner's computer and is used solely to operate the channel. The App does not collect data from any other users, does not use cookies or tracking, and does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Authorization tokens are kept locally until access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time through the Google security settings page. Stored statistics are deleted when no longer needed for operating the channel.
